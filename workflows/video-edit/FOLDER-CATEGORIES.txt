VIDEO EDIT FOLDERS - WHICH CATEGORY EACH ONE BELONGS TO
For color coding the folder icons. Two columns: CATEGORY and STATUS.

CATEGORIES (one color each):
  PREP           - gets the footage ready (transcript, silences, measuring)
  DECIDE AND CUT - decides what stays and where every cut goes
  VEGAS          - builds and checks the VEGAS project
  FINISHING      - sound, picture and effects on the finished cut
  HAND-OFF       - what you get at the end (markers, report, upload text, file)

STATUS (could be a second mark on the icon, e.g. a badge):
  WORKS          - built and used today
  OPT-IN         - built or planned, only switched on for some videos
  NEEDS WORK     - built, but not good enough yet
  NOT BUILT      - nothing yet
